## Project Overview
- This is a research paper project. The paper has various sections:
- **Introduction** - issue at hand. motivation for studying it. previous literature. contribution. structure of the paper.
- **Literature Review** - Reviews relevant literature on the topic - including methodological approaches.
- **Data and Methodology** - Explaining the data used, including preparation and transformations. Explanation of the methodological approach.
- **Results** - Analysing the results and discussion on their meaning
- **Robustness** - Additional analysis to validate the results
- **Conclusion** - Drawing some conclusions and implications. Including some drawbacks
