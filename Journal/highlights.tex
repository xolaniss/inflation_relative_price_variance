\begin{itemize}
    \item[•] We establish a U-shaped, nonlinear relationship between inflation and relative price dispersion in South Africa.
    
    \vspace{0.2cm}
    
    \item[•] Only positive inflation surprises worsen price dispersion; negative surprises have no asymmetric effect.
    
    \vspace{0.2cm}
    
    \item[•] The inflation-RPD nexus evolves with structural changes in inflation targeting regimes.
    
    \vspace{0.2cm}
    
    \item[•] Post-2017, unexpected inflation exhibits a negative, monotonic relationship with price dispersion.
    
    \vspace{0.2cm}
    
    \item[•] Findings support flexible inflation targets that account for nonlinear dynamics in emerging markets.
\end{itemize}
